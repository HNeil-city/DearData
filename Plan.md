# plan

I plan to collect data on the relation of estimated hours spent implementing somehting vs actual hours spent implementing something, as well as the group category of what that something is (for example: DSA, Memory management, etc)

## Data representation

I will form a tree from my data, the order of the data can be shuffled and therefore so can the the tree branches. It will be a thing of where the angle is determined by the ratio of estimate/actual, the length of the brnach determined by the actual time, and the branches will be grouped into categories based on what concept it is that this feature relates to.
